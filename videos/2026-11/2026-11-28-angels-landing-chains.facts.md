## Wording choices (on purpose)
- **"Legend says"** and the card's "attributed to Fisher… 'allegedly quipped'": the NPS permit page says "allegedly", and the NPS Centennial paper (D) quotes Fisher differently ("The Angels would never land on the throne, but would reverently pause at the foot…"). So the one-liner is never presented as his documented words. On-screen quote marks use the NPS wording "there".
- **No switchback count:** D says 21, C says "17 complete switchbacks".
- **No title for Ruesch:** sources call him "acting Supt." (C, Angels Landing form), "Park building foreman" (C, West Rim form) and "first custodian" (D). The video only says he supervised the work.
- **No chain installation date:** no official source found says when the chains went in (C, 1984, only says chains and railings "have been attached to the cliff"). The video says only that the route has chains today.
- **"Chains for handrails"**, not continuous handrails: the drawing shows chains on parts of the half mile (B: "anchored intermittently").
- **No permit dates, odds, daily numbers or quotas:** the only odds and daily figures are in the archived 2024 release; nothing current.
- **The route drawing is an illustration** (side view, not to scale, no elevations; on screen: "Illustration, not to scale").
- End card: "Independent planning service. You apply yourself. Not affiliated with Recreation.gov or the National Park Service."
