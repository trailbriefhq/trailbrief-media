2 permit lotteries close by Tuesday: The Wave and Grand Canyon backcountry. ⏳
#thewavearizona #grandcanyon #hikingpermits #backpacking #arizonahiking

THE WAVE (Coyote Buttes North, BLM): the advance lottery for March 2027 hikes takes applications Nov 1–30. Last day: Mon, Nov 30 (BLM posts no closing time, so don't wait for the last hour). The lottery runs Dec 1 and results come by email. $6 per application, up to 3 dates, groups of up to 6, and $7 per person if you win. Up to 48 people a day get permits this way; 16 more come through the daily lottery. Winners must accept and pay by Dec 15.

GRAND CANYON BACKCOUNTRY (NPS): the early access lottery for trips starting in April 2027 closes Tue, Dec 1 at 5 PM MST (7 PM ET). Applicants are notified Dec 2, and winners get a time slot to book a trip. $10 to apply. You need a permit to camp in the backcountry, including below the rim; day hikes don't.
Heads up: after the Aug 29 flash flood, Bright Angel and Cottonwood campgrounds are closed and not in the lotteries until further notice. Phantom Ranch, the North Kaibab Trail and both river bridges are closed, and drinking water along the trails is off. Check nps.gov/grca before you plan.

Both lotteries are on Recreation.gov. Missed them? The Wave's next window opens Dec 1 (April hikes) and Grand Canyon's opens Dec 16 (May starts).
