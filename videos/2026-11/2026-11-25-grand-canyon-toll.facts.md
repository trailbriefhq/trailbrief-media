## Wording choices (on purpose)
- **"Charging riders"**: NPS says the toll was per mounted user, not per hiker.
- **"Ruled against Cameron"** and **"CLAIM INVALID"**, not "called him a trespasser": "trespasser" is NPS's summary; the Court's opinion holds his claim invalid and affirms the decree.
- **"Dedicated in 1925"**: construction began Dec 1924; NPS gives the June 15, 1925 dedication.
- **Toll "lasted until the Park Service took over" in 1928**: neutral on who collected it (Coconino County held the trail from 1906, with Cameron on its board).
- **"No backcountry permit"** for day hikes: groups of 12–30 and commercial trips have other permit rules.
- **No distances or elevations**, and no permit dates (they live on trailbrief.app).
