## Wording choices (on purpose)
- **"Last day: Mon, Nov 30"**, no closing time: BLM gives none.
- **"Close by Tuesday"**: The Wave on Monday, the Grand Canyon on Tuesday.
- **"Up to 48 people a day"**: the cap is 48 people or 12 groups, whichever comes first.
- **"Winners get a time slot to book"**: the Grand Canyon early access lottery gives a reservation time slot, not a permit.
- **Flood closures named in full** (campgrounds, Phantom Ranch, North Kaibab Trail, both bridges) so nobody applies for a closed area; "Check nps.gov/grca" because closures change.
- **No odds, no "most popular", no counts we didn't read today.**
