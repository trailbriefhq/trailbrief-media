## Wording choices (on purpose)
- **"First recorded climb"**, not "first climb": earlier, unrecorded ascents can't be ruled out.
- **"Three local men"**: the sources say "of Inyo County". The video doesn't claim they were fishermen, only that they wanted the name Fisherman's Peak.
- **No elevation numbers**: official figures differ by survey and datum.
- **No lottery dates in the video**: it posts days after it was made, so the dates live on trailbrief.app instead.
