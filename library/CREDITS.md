# Photo library: credits and rights

Every photo here is a U.S. government work or an archive item marked public domain on the source page (checked Nov 23, 2026). Downloaded once through the built-in browser on Bryce's Mac and resized to 2400 px max with metadata stripped. **Show the credit on screen whenever a photo is used** (e.g. "Photo: NPS / Bill Jones, 1958"), and put it in the video's fact sheet.

Rules for using these:
- Describe a photo only as its source page does. If the page doesn't say which peak is which, don't point at one.
- Historic photos: say the year on screen ("photo: 1929") so nobody mistakes it for today or for the year in the story.
- No recognizable faces: crop or blur them (see notes).
- New photos: Bryce wants to be asked before a video that needs photos not in this library. New downloads come as one bundled file, not one Save box per photo.
